5.5. exec/DSMC – direct simulation Monte Carlo

Purpose

Particle simulation of dilute gas mixtures with direct simulation Monte Carlo, including reservoirs and arbitrary planar surfaces. It serves as a molecular-level reference for the continuum FHD solvers.

Libraries and build

src_particles (through MakeDSMC.package), src_geometry, src_analysis, src_rng, src_common; built with USE_PARTICLES = TRUE, DIM = 3 and MAX_SPEC = 8. Application sources (collisions, particle generation, statistics, plotfiles) are in exec/DSMC. Particle data use AMReX particle containers (The ParticleContainer).

Algorithm

After building the surfaces and initialising particles and collision cells, each time step

  1. selects and performs binary collisions in every collision cell (CalcSelections, CollideParticles);

  2. injects particles at reservoir surfaces (Source);

  3. moves particles and applies surface interactions (MoveParticlesCPP);

  4. accumulates statistics (EvaluateStats), which are reset after n_steps_skip steps.

Species

Each species has a mass, diameter and T_init. The number of simulated particles per species comes from particle_count if it is non-negative, otherwise from the volume fraction phi_domain, otherwise from rho0 and the mass fractions Yk0. particle_neff is the number of real molecules per simulated particle. With particle_input > 0, initial positions are read from particles.dat.

Surfaces

The six domain faces are always surfaces. Their behaviour follows from the boundary-condition parameters (bc_vel_*, bc_therm_*, bc_mass_*, t_lo/hi, n_lo/hi, …); for example, reservoir faces inject particles at a prescribed state. Additional surfaces are read from a file paramplanes.dat in the run directory, if present. Its first entry is the number of extra surfaces, followed by one record per surface:

x0 y0 z0          origin
ux uy uz          first in-plane direction
vx vy vz          second in-plane direction
uTop vTop         extent along u and v
rnx rny rnz       right-side normal
lnx lny lnz       left-side normal
porosity specularity temperature density[nspecies] source sink momentumCons   (right side)
porosity specularity temperature density[nspecies] source sink momentumCons   (left side)
periodicity area

The notebook exec/DSMC/py/paramPlane.ipynb generates this file, and exec/DSMC/py/paramplanes.dat is an example.

Example inputs

inputs_conc: two species under a concentration gradient between mass-conserving reservoirs (bc_vel = 7 in x). inputs_conc_gpu is the GPU variant, and test_inputs/ holds periodic-equilibrium, thermal and concentration tests.

Output

pltcu* (conserved variables, instantaneous and mean), pltprim* (primitive variables per species: number density, density, velocity, temperature, pressure, energy, concentration), pltvar* (variances), pltpart* (particles) and plt_SF_prim* (structure factors).

Related applications

exec/phononDSMC and exec/phononDetector apply the same particle and surface machinery to phonon transport. There, all surfaces come from paramplanes.dat, with no automatic domain faces.
