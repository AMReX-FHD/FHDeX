5.4. exec/reactDiff – stochastic reaction–diffusion

Purpose

Simulates reaction–diffusion systems of species number densities with fluctuations. It implements the fluctuating-hydrodynamics approach to reaction–diffusion described by Kim et al., J. Chem. Phys. 146, 124110 (2017). The inputs files reproduce figures from that paper.

Libraries

src_reactDiff (which provides main_driver), src_chemistry, src_analysis, src_rng, src_common.

Algorithm

Reactions and diffusion are combined either by operator splitting or in a single unsplit step, selected by temporal_integrator:

Value

Scheme

0

D + R (first-order splitting)

1

(1/2)R + D + (1/2)R (Strang splitting, option 1)

2

(1/2)D + R + (1/2)D (Strang splitting, option 2)

-1

unsplit forward Euler

-2

unsplit explicit midpoint

-3

unsplit multinomial diffusion

-4

unsplit implicit midpoint

For split schemes, reactDiff_diffusion_type chooses the diffusion integrator and reactDiff_reaction_type the order of the reaction step; reaction_type selects how reactions are sampled (0 deterministic, 1 CLE, 2 SSA, 3 tau leaping). See Theory and Numerical Methods.

Specifying reactions

Reactions are given by their reactant and product stoichiometries, one pair of lines per reaction, with one entry per species. For example, the reaction \(U + W \to V + W\) in a three-species system is

nspecies  = 3
nreaction = 7
stoich_1R = 1 0 1     # reactants of reaction 1: U + W
stoich_1P = 0 1 1     # products of reaction 1:  V + W
...
rate_const = 0.0002 0.0002 1. 0.03666663 4.44444555555 0.00333333 16.66665
rate_multiplier = 1.
include_discrete_LMA_correction = 1

Rates follow the law of mass action. rate_multiplier scales all of them. With include_discrete_LMA_correction = 1, \(n^2\) and \(n^3\) in the rate expressions become \(n(n-1/\Delta V)\) and \(n(n-1/\Delta V)(n-2/\Delta V)\).

Key parameters

Parameter

Meaning

D_Fick

Fickian diffusion coefficient for each species.

n_init_in_1, n_init_in_2

Initial number densities, interpreted according to prob_type.

integer_populations

Start from integer numbers of molecules per cell.

variance_coef_mass, initial_variance_mass

Stochastic diffusion on/off (1/0) and initial fluctuations.

avg_type

How densities on faces are averaged to weight the stochastic flux (1 arithmetic, 2 geometric, 3 harmonic, 10–12 smoothed Heaviside variants).

bc_mass_lo/hi

-1 periodic, 1 wall (Neumann), 2 reservoir (Dirichlet).

cell_depth

Out-of-plane thickness in 2D; sets the cell volume and hence the noise strength.
Example inputs

inputs_paper_BPM_2d (Baras–Pearson–Mansour limit cycle, Figures 5–7 of the paper), inputs_paper_Lemarchand_3d (Figure 8), and test_Schlogl_2d/ (Schlögl model, with job scripts).

Output

Plotfiles plt* with the number densities of all species every plot_int steps; structure factors when struct_fact_int > 0.
